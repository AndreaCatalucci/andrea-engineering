# Non-Software Facilitation

Use the parent skill's dependency-ordered interview unchanged. This reference adds only domain-specific judgment for non-software decisions.

## Adjust the Lens

- For personal or high-stakes choices, establish values, feelings, and reversibility before optimizing logistics.
- For creative work, favor energy, distinctiveness, and useful constraints over exhaustive risk analysis.
- When several people are affected, identify whose preferences and authority differ; alignment may be the real decision.
- If the user lacks domain knowledge, map the territory before asking them to choose within it.
- Treat goals, preferences, experience, and interpersonal context as user-held facts. Ask for them openly; do not recommend or infer their answers.

## Explore Creatively

Use inversion, constraint removal, analogy, and adjacent possibilities to discover meaningful options. Give one recommendation and at most two alternatives for each material decision. Do not produce a detached idea dump.

## Converge

Stop when all choices that materially affect the desired outcome are resolved. Present the same final shared-understanding synthesis and wait for explicit confirmation.

This route does not use the software plan metadata or write under `docs/plans/` automatically. After confirmation, offer only relevant choices:

- hand the confirmed direction to `ce-plan`;
- save a summary when the user requests it;
- publish through `ce-proof` when requested and available;
- finish with the confirmed synthesis in chat.

Do not create or hand off anything before confirmation.
