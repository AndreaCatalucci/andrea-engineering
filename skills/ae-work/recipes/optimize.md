# Optimize

Use when success is a measurable improvement.

Name one primary metric, its current baseline, the target or completion criterion, and constraints that remain stable. Observable measurement is the entry condition; surface the missing measurement when it is unavailable.

Change one coherent factor at a time. Measure with the same method, keep improvements, and revert regressions. Record the experiment, result, and next decision.

Complete the run when the target is met, the agreed budget is exhausted, or further progress requires a product or architecture decision. Return the baseline, final result, retained changes, and remaining constraint.
