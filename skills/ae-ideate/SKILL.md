# Ideate

The user has a problem and no solution yet. Generate ideas through independent synthesis.

1. Read the request and the relevant local context.
2. Ground the exploration in observed facts from the best available sources: social media, news, and search.
3. Through Impact Mapping, name the goal in outcome terms, the people who can help or hinder it, the behavior changes that would achieve it, and 3-7 genuinely different options that could cause those changes. Draw options from distinct branches. Include the obvious direction and at least one that challenges the premise when useful. The map is working material for the brief.

Load [`wardley.md`](recipes/wardley.md) when capability evolution, commoditization, ecosystem structure, build-vs-buy, or differentiation materially affects the direction.

4. State consequential assumptions rather than asking.
5. Compare the options on the few dimensions that matter here: value, effort, risk, and reversibility.
6. Write an Opportunity Brief under `docs/ideation/` covering:

- problem or opportunity;
- people;
- evidence;
- current thesis: the strongest path through the map;
- alternatives;
- riskiest assumptions;
- open questions.

Distinguish facts from inference. Point to sources rather than restating them. Keep implementation detail proportional to the decision.

The brief is the deliverable, including when the thesis is not to pursue the idea.

Before finishing, run the `unslop` skill.
