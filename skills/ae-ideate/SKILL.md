# Ideate

Explore the user's problem through independent synthesis.

1. Read the request and the relevant local context.
2. Ground the exploration in observed facts from the best available sources: social media, news, and search.
3. State consequential assumptions rather than asking.
4. Generate 3-7 genuinely different options. Include the obvious direction and at least one that challenges the premise when useful.
5. Compare them on the few dimensions that matter here: value, effort, risk, and reversibility.
6. Recommend the strongest option or combination and name the tradeoff.

Distinguish facts from inference. Keep implementation detail proportional to the decision.

Before finishing, run the `unslop` skill.
